# Humanizer Skill: Natural Prose & Anti-AI Tells Elimination

## Overview
This skill eliminates the characteristic structural patterns, synthetic rhetorical devices, and stylistic giveaways commonly produced by large language models. The objective is to ensure prose feels observed, tactile, and written by an authentic human voice.

## Forbidden AI Structural Patterns

### 1. The "Not X, but Y" Construction
- **Forbidden Pattern**: Framing narrative descriptions or character revelations through binary contrast (e.g., *"It was not just a library; it was a sanctuary,"* or *"This was not about revenge, but about survival."*).
- **Rule**: Eliminate the negative setup completely. State the concrete truth directly: *"The library had three-story shelves of mildewed calfskin."*

### 2. Isolated Dramatic Closers & Trailing Truisms
- **Forbidden Pattern**: Concluding paragraphs or chapters with isolated, melodramatic one-liners meant to force gravitas:
  - *"The game had begun."*
  - *"That was the real danger."*
  - *"And nothing would ever be the same."*
  - *"Little did he know what awaited him."*
- **Rule**: End on concrete sensory action, unresolved physical stillness, or an interrupted gesture. Allow the dramatic stakes to arise organically from the scene rather than announcer-style summaries.

### 3. Pseudo-Profound Aphorisms
- **Forbidden Pattern**: Inserting fake philosophical epigrams masquerading as wisdom:
  - *"At its core, grief is just love with nowhere to go."*
  - *"Silence was the truest weapon in his arsenal."*
  - *"Trust is a currency spent only once."*
- **Rule**: Ground character thoughts in specific, messy, tactical considerations rather than fortune-cookie philosophy.

### 4. Forced Triads
- **Forbidden Pattern**: Mechanically arranging nouns, adjectives, or clauses into sets of three for artificial rhythm:
  - *"grief, guilt, and vengeance"*
  - *"cold, dark, and merciless"*
  - *"He checked his watch, adjusted his coat, and stepped into the storm."*
- **Rule**: Break symmetric triples. Use pairs, asymmetric singles, or concrete physical sequences with irregular cadence.

### 5. Staged Run-Ups & Narrator Throat-Clearing
- **Forbidden Pattern**: Over-explaining transitions before showing the action:
  - *"To understand why Julian hesitated, one must look at his past."*
  - *"It was a decision that would haunt him for years."*
- **Rule**: Begin immediately in media res. Present the scene's physical environment and character action without meta-commentary.

### 6. Phantom Objections
- **Forbidden Pattern**: The narrator defensively debating an imaginary reader:
  - *"This was not to say that she was incapable of mercy..."*
  - *"Don't mistake his silence for weakness..."*
- **Rule**: Cut defensive hedging. Portray behavior and trust the reader to interpret it.

## Authentic Dialogue Directives
1. **Human Speech Rhythm**: Real humans hesitate, use incomplete fragments, change their minds mid-sentence, and rarely deliver structured essays.
2. **Subtext Over Exposition**: Characters must avoid announcing their feelings or backstories in dialogue. What is left unsaid between speakers carries more tension than what is said out loud.
3. **Physical Counterpoint**: During conversation, ground participants in physical objects and mundane activities (cutting an apple, checking a pocket, closing a window) rather than theatrical facial expressions.
